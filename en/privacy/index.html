Privacy policy

Last updated October 16, 2026

1. Who handles your data

The controller of your personal data is Laysa Santiago Alfredo, pessoa física. For anything about privacy — including reaching our data protection officer — write to oficialfanme@gmail.com.

This policy explains in plain language what data Fanme uses, why, who it is shared with and how you control it, under Brazil’s General Data Protection Law (LGPD, Law 13,709/2018).

2. Data we use

Account: email, password (stored encrypted — not even we can see it), birth date, username, name, bio, profile photo, cover, chosen language and theme. If you sign in with Google, we receive that account’s email and name.

What you create and do in the app: stories, characters, your Fanmes, AI conversations, GIFs you send, comments, likes, who you follow and block, saved messages, reports you make and problem reports (with your OS and app version).

Technical data: access logs (such as IP address, date and time), AI usage counters and AI call records — provider, model, response time, success and amount of text processed, without the conversation content. If the app crashes, an error report with device model, OS, app version and the point in the code that failed.

We don’t collect location, contacts, your photo library (only the images you choose to upload), biometrics or advertising identifiers. We don’t show ads.

3. Why we use it and on what legal basis

To provide the service you signed up for (LGPD, art. 7, V): create and keep your account, generate AI replies, show stories, comments and profiles, sync your conversations across devices, email verification codes and process the Fanme+ subscription.

Legitimate interest (art. 7, IX), always with as little data as possible: keeping the app secure, preventing abuse and fraud, enforcing usage limits, moderating reports, fixing bugs and understanding usage in aggregate to improve Fanme.

Legal obligations (art. 7, II): keeping access logs for the period required by Brazil’s Internet Civil Framework and complying with orders from authorities.

Consent (art. 7, I): Fanme news notifications, which you can turn off anytime in Settings → Notifications.

The order of stories on Home is the same for everyone: we don’t build a taste profile to personalize what you see or for advertising.

4. How the AI uses your conversations

To write each reply, we send the AI provider only what is needed: the story and character details, your Fanme’s name and description and the most recent messages of the conversation. Your email and password are never sent.

We use providers such as Cerebras, Groq, Google (Gemini) and OpenRouter, which may route the request to other models. Each handles data under its own policy; some providers may keep the text for a period for safety or to improve their services. So don’t write real personal data (such as address, phone or ID numbers) in your conversations.

We don’t sell your conversations and we don’t use them to train AI models.

5. Who we share data with

Only those we need for the app to work, who process data on our behalf: Supabase (hosting, database and image storage); AI providers (section 4); KLIPY (GIF search — receives the search term and an anonymous identifier); Google (sending code emails); Sentry (error reports); and, for subscriptions, the payment provider or app store.

We may also share data when required by law or by order of a competent authority. We never sell personal data.

6. International transfers

Some of these services are outside Brazil, mainly in the United States. In those cases the transfer is made to deliver the service you asked for, with providers that adopt safeguards compatible with the LGPD (art. 33).

7. What is public and what is private

Visible to other people signed in to Fanme: your profile (name, username, bio, photo, cover and follower counts), the stories you publish and the comments you post.

Private: your AI conversations, your likes, your Fanmes, your saved messages, your private stories and the copies you make of other people’s stories.

8. Children and teens

Fanme is for people 13 and over. We handle teens’ data in their best interest (LGPD, art. 14): we collect the minimum, show no ads and don’t use data for advertising or profiling. "Adult" stories are for people 18 and over only.

We ask for your birth date at sign-up only to confirm the minimum age and hide "Adult" stories from anyone under 18. It isn’t shown on your profile, nobody else sees it and it can’t be changed later — to fix a mistake, write to the contact email.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child under 13 created an account, or want to exercise rights on behalf of a teen, write to the contact email.

9. How long we keep data

We keep your data while your account exists. When you delete your account (Settings → Delete account), we erase your account and profile data, conversations, comments, likes, follows, Fanmes, saved messages and your images.

Exceptions: public stories other people were already playing stay available to them, without your name — along with those stories’ images — so their conversations don’t disappear; AI call records remain with no link to you; access logs are kept for 6 months as Brazilian law requires; and data may be kept when the law requires it. Backups are replaced automatically within a short time.

10. How we protect it

Encrypted connections, encrypted password storage, access rules that let each person see and change only what is theirs, service keys kept only on the server and your session protected in your device’s secure storage. No system is 100% secure; if an incident could put you at risk, we will notify you and Brazil’s data protection authority (ANPD).

11. Your rights

Under the LGPD (art. 18) you can: confirm whether we process your data and access it; correct incomplete or wrong data; ask for anonymization, blocking or deletion of unnecessary data; ask for portability; know who we share it with; withdraw consent; and object to processing that doesn’t follow the law.

You can do a lot on your own in the app: edit your profile, delete messages, stories and comments, change privacy and delete your account. For anything else, write to oficialfanme@gmail.com with the subject "[PRIVACY]" — we reply within 15 days. You can also complain to Brazil’s National Data Protection Authority (ANPD) at www.gov.br/anpd.

12. Storage on your device

The app stores on your device only your session (in secure storage) and preferences such as language and theme. We don’t use advertising cookies or third-party trackers.

13. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy in a significant way, we will let you know in the app at least 15 days before it takes effect. The date of the last update is shown at the top.
