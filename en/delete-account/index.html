Delete account

In the app

Open Fanme → Profile → Settings → Delete account, and confirm by typing your username. Deletion is immediate.

By email

If you can’t sign in, write to oficialfanme@gmail.com with the subject "[DELETE ACCOUNT]" from your account’s email. We reply within 15 days.

What is deleted

Account and profile data, birth date, AI conversations, comments, likes, follows, Fanmes, saved messages and your images.

What stays

Public stories other people were already playing stay available to them, without your name. Access logs are kept for 6 months as Brazilian law requires, and AI usage records remain with no link to you.
